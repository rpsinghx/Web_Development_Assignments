Destination Overview

Welcome to Jammu

Jammu is a beautiful destination in northern India known for its temples, historic palaces, scenic landscapes and rich Dogra culture.

The city is popularly known as the City of Temples and serves as an important gateway to several famous destinations in the Jammu region.

Some of the most popular attractions include Vaishno Devi Mata Mandir, Bahu Fort, Hari Niwas Palace, Amar Mahal Palace and Mansar Lake.

Visitors can enjoy temple visits, heritage walks, nature sightseeing, photography, local food and traditional shopping.

Vaishno Devi Mata Mandir

A Major Pilgrimage Destination

Vaishno Devi Mata Mandir is one of the most important pilgrimage destinations in the Jammu region. The shrine is located in the Trikuta Hills near Katra.

The pilgrimage attracts visitors throughout the year and is known for its spiritual importance and scenic mountain surroundings.

Visitors should check the official shrine board website for current registration, travel and visitor information.

Hari Niwas Palace

Royal Heritage of Jammu

Hari Niwas Palace is a historic palace located in Jammu. The palace is associated with the Dogra royal heritage and overlooks the Tawi River valley.

The palace is known for its heritage architecture, beautiful surroundings and connection with the history of Jammu.

It is a suitable destination for visitors interested in architecture, history and the royal heritage of Jammu.
