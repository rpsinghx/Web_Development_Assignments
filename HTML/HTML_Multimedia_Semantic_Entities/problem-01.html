Admission Process

  1. Register online at official portal
  2. Complete JEE Main Examination (All India Test)
  3. Score validation & Document Verification
  4. Seat Allocation through JOSAA Counselling
  5. Online Admission Confirmation & Fee Submission
  6. Document Collection at Campus
  7. Hostel Allocation & Orientation Programme
  8. Commencement of Academic Session

Important Information

Eligibility
Applicants must have passed 12th standard with minimum 75% aggregate in PCM subjects from recognized board
Scholarship
Merit-based scholarships covering up to 100% tuition fees available for deserving candidates; SC/ST/OBC candidates eligible for reserved seats
Merit List
Prepared based on JEE Main All India Rank (AIR); merit lists released on official website in phases
Application Fee
Registration fee: ₹1,500 for General & OBC categories; ₹750 for SC/ST/PwD categories; fee non-refundable
