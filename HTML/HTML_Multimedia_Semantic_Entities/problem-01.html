Welcome to Harvard University

Welcome to the Harvard University Digital Campus & Admissions Portal. This portal provides prospective students with information about academics, campus life, admission procedures and student opportunities.

Harvard University was founded in 1636 and is located in Cambridge, Massachusetts.

The university brings together students, faculty, researchers and communities from around the world.

About Harvard University

History and Academic Community

Harvard University is one of the world's oldest institutions of higher education. Its academic community includes students and scholars working across a wide range of disciplines.

Harvard encourages students to develop critical thinking, creativity, research skills and leadership abilities.

Students can participate in academic courses, research projects, student organizations, cultural activities and community programs.

Harvard Yard

Harvard Yard contains historic buildings, pathways and green spaces that form an important part of the university's campus.

Harvard Campus Environment

Harvard's campus includes historic architecture, libraries, classrooms, laboratories and spaces for student activities.

Admissions

Admission Announcement

Important Admission Notice: Prospective students should carefully review the official admission requirements, deadlines and application instructions before applying.

Applicants should ensure that all information submitted through the application is complete, accurate and up to date.

Admission requirements can vary depending on the academic program and applicant category.

Admission Process

  1. Explore available academic programs.
  2. Review eligibility requirements.
  3. Research admission deadlines.
  4. Prepare academic and personal information.
  5. Complete the application form.
  6. Review all information before submission.
  7. Submit the application.
  8. Monitor official admission communications.

Application Guidance

Helpful Application Tips

Applicants should begin preparing their application well before the deadline.

  • Keep academic records ready.
  • Check application requirements carefully.
  • Use a valid email address.
  • Keep important dates in a calendar.
  • Review the application before submitting it.
  • Keep a copy of submitted information.
  • Follow official university communication.
