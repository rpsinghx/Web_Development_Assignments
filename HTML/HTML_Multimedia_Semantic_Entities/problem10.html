Welcome to the National Tech & Career Expo

The National Tech & Career Expo 2026 is a major platform designed to connect students, job seekers, technology enthusiasts and industry professionals with leading organizations.

The expo provides opportunities for career guidance, internships, placements, workshops, networking and technical learning.

The event brings together companies from sectors such as Artificial Intelligence, Software Development, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity, Data Science, FinTech and Consulting.

Event Overview

About the Expo

The National Tech & Career Expo is designed to help participants understand the changing technology industry and discover new career opportunities.

Visitors can attend expert sessions, interact with recruiters, participate in workshops and explore opportunities offered by leading technology organizations.

The event focuses on practical learning, professional development and meaningful industry interaction.

Organizer's Message

Dr. Alexander Bennett, Event Director, welcomes students and professionals to the expo.

"Technology creates opportunities when knowledge is combined with curiosity, practical skills and continuous learning."

Participants are encouraged to ask questions, build professional connections and make the most of every learning opportunity.

Featured Speakers

Dr. Alexander Bennett

Dr. Alexander Bennett is a technology strategist specializing in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Digital Transformation.

He will discuss the future of AI and the skills required to build successful technology careers.

Ethan Williams

Ethan Williams is an industry professional and career mentor who works with students and young professionals.

His session focuses on technical skills, communication skills, professional confidence and career planning.

Sophia Anderson

Sophia Anderson is a recruitment and career specialist who helps students understand modern hiring practices.

She will conduct an interactive session on resume preparation, interviews and professional communication.

How to Prepare for the Expo

  • Research participating organizations.
  • Prepare an updated resume.
  • Review your technical skills.
  • Prepare a short professional introduction.
  • Practice common interview questions.
  • Carry your student or professional identification.
  • Prepare questions for recruiters and speakers.
  • Wear appropriate professional attire.
  • Keep your portfolio or project links ready.
  • Maintain professional communication.

Attendee Journey

  1. Complete online registration.
  2. Receive your event confirmation.
  3. Arrive at the venue and complete check-in.
  4. Collect your event badge.
  5. Attend the opening ceremony.
  6. Participate in workshops and expert sessions.
  7. Visit participating company booths.
  8. Interact with recruiters and professionals.
  9. Attend networking sessions.
  10. Follow up with useful professional contacts.

Career Terminology

Career Expo
An organized event where students, professionals and organizations interact about career opportunities.
Internship
A temporary professional learning opportunity that provides practical experience.
Placement
A recruitment opportunity through which students or graduates can obtain employment.
Workshop
An interactive learning session focused on a particular skill or subject.
Certification
An official recognition showing that a person has completed specific learning or skill requirements.
Networking
The process of building professional relationships with individuals and organizations.
