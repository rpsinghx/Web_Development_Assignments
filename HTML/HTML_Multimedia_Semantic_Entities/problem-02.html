Recruitment & Hiring Stages

Our transparent & fair hiring process ensures we find the best talent

  1. Application & Screening: Submit resume & cover letter; initial screening by HR team
  2. Phone Interview: Preliminary discussion about experience & motivation (×30 minutes)
  3. Technical Assessment: Coding test or technical evaluation as per role requirements
  4. Technical Interview Round 1: In-depth problem-solving & algorithm discussion
  5. Technical Interview Round 2: System design & advanced technical concepts evaluation
  6. Behavioral Interview: Discussion on soft skills, teamwork & leadership qualities
  7. Executive Interview: Conversation with senior leadership & team fit assessment
  8. Offer & Negotiation: Final offer with package discussion & contract signing

Hiring Information & Details

Screening Process:
All applications reviewed within 2 weeks; shortlisted candidates contacted via email or phone. We use automated & manual screening to identify top candidates.
Technical Round:
Focuses on data structures, algorithms & system design. Candidates work on real-world problems. Duration: ×90 minutes — ₹50,000 performance incentive for selected candidates.
HR Round:
Assessment of cultural fit, communication skills & alignment with Google values. Discussion about team dynamics, conflict resolution & leadership potential.
Offer Letter:
Comprehensive package including base salary, stock options & performance bonuses. Benefits begin day one of employment. Standard contract term: ≥ 2 years preferred.
