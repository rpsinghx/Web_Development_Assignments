Bank Accounts

Savings Account

A savings account is designed to help individuals store money safely while earning interest on eligible balances.

It can be useful for emergency funds, short-term goals and regular savings.

Tip: Keep a portion of your income reserved for unexpected expenses.

Current Account

A current account is generally designed for frequent transactions. It is commonly used by businesses and organizations that need regular deposits and payments.

Always check applicable minimum-balance requirements and service charges before opening an account.

Fixed Deposit

A fixed deposit allows money to be placed with a bank for a selected period at a predetermined interest rate.

The interest earned depends on the deposited amount, applicable rate and selected tenure.

Understanding Bank Cards

Debit Card

A debit card allows customers to make payments using money available in their linked bank account.

  • Useful for everyday purchases.
  • Can be used for online payments.
  • Transactions normally reduce available account balance.
  • Never share your PIN or one-time password.

Credit Card

A credit card provides a line of credit that can be used for eligible purchases.

Paying the outstanding amount according to the card's terms can help avoid additional interest and charges.

Important: A credit limit is not the same as available income.

Understanding Loans

Personal Loan

A personal loan is a form of borrowing that may be used for eligible personal expenses. The borrower generally repays the principal and applicable interest through scheduled payments.

Education Loan

Education loans can help eligible students or their families finance approved education-related expenses, subject to lender terms and eligibility conditions.

Home Loan

A home loan is generally used to finance the purchase, construction or improvement of eligible residential property.

Because home loans may have long repayment periods, borrowers should carefully consider the total repayment amount before committing.

Important Financial Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage used to calculate interest charged on borrowing or earned on certain deposits.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment is a scheduled payment generally used to repay a loan over time.
Principal
The original amount borrowed or deposited before applicable interest or returns.
Tenure
The period for which a loan, deposit or financial product remains active according to its terms.

Responsible Personal Finance Tips

10 Useful Financial Habits

  • Create a monthly budget.
  • Track regular expenses.
  • Save a portion of available income.
  • Build an emergency fund gradually.
  • Understand fees before using a financial product.
  • Pay bills and loan instalments on time.
  • Avoid borrowing more than you can reasonably repay.
  • Protect banking passwords and authentication details.
  • Review financial goals regularly.
  • Read product terms and conditions carefully.

Security Notice

Never share your OTP, PIN, CVV, password or banking credentials with another person.

Banks and legitimate financial institutions should not require you to reveal confidential authentication information through unsolicited calls or messages.

Steps to Open a Bank Account

  1. Choose an account type according to your needs.
  2. Review eligibility requirements and applicable charges.
  3. Complete the bank's application process.
  4. Provide the required information and documents.
  5. Complete applicable verification procedures.
  6. Read the account terms carefully.
  7. Activate your account and available banking services.

Basic Financial Calculation

Simple Interest

A basic simple-interest calculation can be represented as:

SI = (P × R × T) ÷ 100

Where P represents Principal, R represents Rate and T represents Time.

Example: If P = ₹10,000, R = 5% and T = 2 years, then:

SI = (10,000 × 5 × 2) ÷ 100 = ₹1,000
