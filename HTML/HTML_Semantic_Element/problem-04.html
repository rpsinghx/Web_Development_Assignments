MacBook Pro 16-inch (Apple M4 Pro / M4 Max)

Festive Price Drop — ₹2,79,900 ₹2,49,900 on the base configuration.

Overview

The MacBook Pro 16-inch is Apple's most powerful laptop, built for creators, developers, and professionals who need serious performance in a portable form. Powered by the SoC Apple M4 Pro or M4 Max, it pairs a stunning Liquid Retina XDR display with all-day battery life.

Whether you're compiling code, editing 8K video, or rendering 3D scenes, this configuration is engineered to keep up without slowing down.

Key Features

  • Apple M4 Pro or M4 Max chip with up to a 16-core CPU
  • 16.2-inch Liquid Retina XDR display with ProMotion (up to 120Hz)
  • Up to 24-hour battery life1
  • 1080p FaceTime HD camera with Center Stage
  • Six-speaker sound system with Spatial Audio
  • MagSafe 3 charging plus three Thunderbolt 5 ports

Technical Notes

You can verify the exact chip and installed memory at any time from macOS Terminal:

$ system_profiler SPHardwareDataType | grep "Chip\|Memory"
  Chip: Apple M4 Pro
  Memory: 24 GB

The built-in battery uses a LiCoO2-based lithium-ion cell, chosen for its high energy density relative to its weight.

1 Battery life varies by use and configuration; testing was conducted under simulated wireless web-browsing conditions.

Specifications at a Glance

RAM
Unified memory shared between the CPU and GPU; it determines how many apps and browser tabs run smoothly at once. Available up to 48GB.
SSD
Solid State Drive — flash-based storage with no moving parts, giving fast boot and load times. Available up to 2TB.
Refresh Rate
The ProMotion display adapts up to 120Hz, making scrolling and cursor movement noticeably smoother.
Warranty
Every unit ships with a 1-year limited hardware warranty; extended AppleCare+ coverage is available at checkout.
