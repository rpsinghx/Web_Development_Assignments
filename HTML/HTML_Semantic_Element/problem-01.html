Welcome to the National Defence Academy

The National Defence Academy (NDA) is a premier tri-service training institution located at Khadakwasla, Pune, Maharashtra.

NDA provides joint training to cadets of the Indian Army, Indian Navy and Indian Air Force. The academy focuses on academic education, physical fitness, military training, discipline and leadership development.

The three-year training programme is divided into six academic terms and develops cadets academically, physically and professionally.

NDA Campus & Cadet Life

NDA Training Wings

The National Defence Academy provides joint training for future officers of the three services.

  • Army Wing - Training for future officers of the Indian Army.
  • Navy Wing - Training for future officers of the Indian Navy.
  • Air Force Wing - Training for future officers of the Indian Air Force.
  • Naval Academy 10+2 Entry - Entry route associated with the NDA & NA examination.

NDA Eligibility

Educational Qualification

Candidates applying for the Army Wing must have passed or be appearing in the 12th class or an equivalent examination.

Candidates applying for the Navy, Air Force and Naval Academy routes must have studied the prescribed subjects, including Physics and Mathematics.

Age Requirement

Candidates must satisfy the prescribed date-of-birth conditions mentioned in the relevant UPSC NDA notification.

Other Conditions

  • Candidate must satisfy nationality requirements.
  • Candidate must satisfy the prescribed marital-status conditions.
  • Candidate must satisfy the applicable physical standards.
  • Candidate must qualify through the prescribed selection process.

Always check the latest UPSC notification because eligibility conditions may change between examination cycles.

NDA Application Process

Candidates must complete the application process through the official UPSC online system.

  1. Read the latest NDA examination notification.
  2. Check your eligibility.
  3. Visit the official UPSC application portal.
  4. Complete the registration process.
  5. Enter personal and educational details.
  6. Select the required examination centre.
  7. Upload the required information and documents.
  8. Pay the applicable examination fee, if required.
  9. Review the application carefully.
  10. Submit and save the application.
