About Google

Google is a technology company that develops products and services used by people around the world. Its work includes internet services, software, hardware, cloud computing, artificial intelligence and many other technologies.

Google has offices in several Indian cities including Bengaluru, Gurugram, Hyderabad, Mumbai and Pune.

The company provides opportunities across engineering, artificial intelligence, cloud, security, design, data, marketing, sales and other professional areas.

Employee Value Statement: Google employees work on challenging problems while collaborating with people from different technical and professional backgrounds.

Technology can help people solve problems and create opportunities around the world.

Open Positions

The following are example job categories suitable for a Google Careers recruitment portal.

Software Engineer

Software engineers design, develop, test and maintain software systems and contribute to large-scale technology products.

Skills: Python, Java, C++, algorithms, data structures and software development.

AI / Machine Learning Engineer

AI and Machine Learning engineers work on intelligent systems, machine learning models and AI infrastructure.

Skills: Python, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, statistics and model development.

Android Software Engineer

Android engineers develop mobile applications and technologies for Android devices and services.

Skills: Java, Kotlin, Android development and software engineering.

Cloud Engineer

Cloud engineers work with infrastructure, networking, distributed systems and cloud technologies.

Skills: Cloud Computing, Linux, networking, Python and distributed systems.

Cybersecurity Engineer

Cybersecurity professionals help protect applications, infrastructure, systems and information.

Skills: Network Security, programming, authentication and security analysis.

UX / Interaction Designer

UX designers create user experiences and interfaces for digital products and services.

Skills: UX design, interaction design, prototyping and visual communication.

Data Analyst

Data analysts examine information and provide insights that help teams make informed decisions.

Skills: SQL, statistics, data visualization and analytical thinking.

Hiring Process

Candidates can search for a suitable position, review its qualifications and submit an application through Google Careers.

Hiring Stages

  1. Search for a suitable job
  2. Review job requirements
  3. Prepare your resume
  4. Submit your application
  5. Application screening
  6. Interview process
  7. Final decision
  8. Offer and joining process

Recruitment Terms

Screening
Recruiters review the candidate's application, qualifications and experience.
Technical Round
Technical candidates may be evaluated on programming, problem-solving and role-specific technical knowledge.
HR / Behavioral Round
Candidates may discuss teamwork, communication, experience and professional behavior.
Offer
Successful candidates receive information regarding the position and employment terms.

Recruiter Tips

  • Read the complete job description carefully.
  • Apply only for positions that match your skills.
  • Keep your resume updated.
  • Highlight relevant projects and achievements.
  • Check qualification requirements before applying.
  • Review your application before submitting it.
