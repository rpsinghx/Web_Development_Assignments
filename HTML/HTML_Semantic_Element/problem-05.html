Event Overview

The National Career & Education Fair (NCEF) is a two-day event bringing together corporates, government bodies, universities, and scholarship providers to guide students and job seekers through their next career step. Now in its 10th edition, NCEF has grown into one of the country's largest student-facing career events.

Whether you are a final-year student, a recent graduate, or someone considering a career change, NCEF gives you direct access to recruiters, mentors, and scholarship counsellors — all free of cost for registered attendees.

As part of our sustainability pledge, this edition is organised as a CO2-neutral event, with all attendee kits printed on recycled paper.

Objectives

  • Connect students and job seekers directly with hiring organizations
  • Provide accurate, up-to-date information on internships, placements, and apprenticeships
  • Guide students towards relevant scholarships and higher-education pathways
  • Offer practical preparation support — resume reviews, mock interviews, and career counselling

Every year, NCEF is built around one simple idea: no student should miss an opportunity simply because they didn't know it existed.

— Fair Director, NCEF 2026

Venue & Logistics

NCEF 2026 will be held at Pragati Maidan, New Delhi, Halls A–D, with dedicated zones for IT, Banking & Finance, Core Engineering, and Government & Scholarships. Entry is via Gate 4, and attendees are advised to carry a printed or digital copy of their registration confirmation for quick check-in.

Preparation Guide

A little preparation goes a long way at a fair this size. Below is the typical attendee journey, followed by our top preparation tips.

  1. Pre-register online and download your entry pass.
  2. Prepare and print multiple copies of your resume.
  3. Arrive early and check in at Gate 4 using your QR code.
  4. Visit booths in your preferred sector zone first.
  5. Attend at least one workshop or panel session.
  6. Collect contact details and follow up within a week.

Preparation Tips

  • Carry a valid photo ID along with your registration confirmation.
  • Dress in business casual attire — most recruiters expect it.
  • Research shortlisted organizations beforehand using the table below.
  • Keep a soft copy of your resume on your phone as a backup.
  • Prepare a short 30-second introduction about yourself.

Important Notices

  • Session timings are subject to change; check on-site screens for updates.
  • Outside food and large bags are not permitted inside the halls.

Information & FAQs

Terminology

Career Fair
An organized event where employers, institutions, and job seekers meet to exchange information about opportunities.
Internship
A short-term, often paid, work assignment that lets students gain practical experience in a chosen field.
Placement
The process by which an educational institution or fair connects a graduating student with a full-time job offer.
Scholarship
Financial assistance awarded to students, usually based on merit, need, or a specific eligibility criterion, that does not need to be repaid.
Apprenticeship
A structured programme combining on-the-job training with classroom instruction, typically leading to a recognised qualification.
