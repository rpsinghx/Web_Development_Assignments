About Shalby Hospitals

Shalby Hospitals is a multispecialty healthcare group that began in 1994 as an orthopedic centre. It has grown into a healthcare network offering medical services across multiple specialties and locations in India.

The Ahmedabad SG Highway hospital is one of Shalby's flagship facilities and provides a wide range of clinical services, diagnostic facilities and emergency care.

Shalby follows a patient-centric approach and focuses on clinical excellence and quality healthcare practices.

"Committed to strict clinical protocols and best-in-class healthcare practices to provide the best possible outcomes."

Shalby Hospital SG Highway is located opposite Karnavati Club on S.G. Road, Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

Operating Information

Emergency services: Available 24 × 7.

General OPD: Shalby's official website states that OPD consultations are generally available from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and patients are advised to take a prior appointment with the concerned doctor.

For emergencies, patients should contact the appropriate Shalby hospital emergency service rather than waiting for a routine OPD appointment.

Common Healthcare Terms

Consultation
A planned discussion and examination with an appropriate healthcare professional.
Follow-up
A subsequent consultation after an earlier visit or treatment.
OPD
Outpatient Department where patients receive consultation without routine hospital admission.
Appointment
A scheduled time requested for consultation or another healthcare service.

How to Request an Appointment

  1. Select the appropriate department.
  2. Choose a preferred doctor or specialist.
  3. Select a suitable date and preferred time.
  4. Provide your basic contact information.
  5. Describe the general reason for requesting the visit.
  6. Submit the appointment request.
  7. Wait for confirmation from the hospital.

You can also visit the official Shalby OPD Enquiry page.

Patient Instructions & Support

  • Carry previous medical records when relevant.
  • Carry identification and appointment information.
  • Arrive before the scheduled consultation time.
  • Follow hospital staff instructions during your visit.
  • Confirm doctor availability before travelling for a planned consultation.
