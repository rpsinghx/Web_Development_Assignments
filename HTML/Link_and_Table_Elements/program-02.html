Indian Accent , New Delhi

Welcome to Indian Accent

Indian Accent is a renowned modern Indian restaurant located at The Lodhi, Lodhi Road, New Delhi. The restaurant presents Indian flavours and traditions using contemporary techniques and global ingredients. Its menu includes à la carte dishes as well as tasting-menu experiences.

The restaurant also offers vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free menu options.

Special Offer: Enjoy a complimentary dessert with selected tasting-menu experiences.


Starters

  • Potato Chaat with Black Truffle - A contemporary interpretation of the popular Indian street-food classic.
  • Mushroom Kulcha - Soft Indian bread filled with a flavorful mushroom preparation.
  • Sweet Corn Shorba - A warm and comforting soup prepared with sweet corn and aromatic spices.
  • Butter Chicken Kulcha - A modern combination of butter chicken flavours and Indian bread.

Main Course

  • Malai Kofta - Soft vegetable and paneer dumplings served with a rich Indian gravy.
  • Five-Spice Squash Curry - A vegetarian curry prepared with squash and aromatic spices.
  • Green Jackfruit XO Balchao - A contemporary preparation combining Indian flavours with global culinary influences.
  • Chef's Tasting Menu - A multi-course dining experience showcasing the restaurant's inventive Indian cuisine.

Desserts

  • Doda Barfi - A modern presentation of the traditional Indian sweet.
  • Single Origin Manam Chocolate Cake - Chocolate cake served with basundi and berries.
  • Basundi with Berries - Creamy Indian-style sweetened milk accompanied by fresh berries.
  • Seasonal Indian Dessert - A dessert prepared according to seasonal ingredients.

Beverages

  • Indian Tea Selection - A selection of Indian teas to complement the meal.
  • Fresh Lime Beverage - A refreshing citrus-based drink.
  • Seasonal Fruit Beverage - A refreshing drink prepared using seasonal fruits.
  • Indian Accent Signature Beverage - A specially prepared beverage recommended by the restaurant.

Chef's Recommended Dining Experience

Follow this sequence for a complete dining experience:
  1. Begin with Potato Chaat with Black Truffle as an appetizer.
  2. Continue with Mushroom Kulcha or Sweet Corn Shorba.
  3. Enjoy a selection of contemporary Indian main-course dishes.
  4. Pair the meal with a suitable beverage.
  5. Finish the experience with Single Origin Manam Chocolate Cake, Basundi and Berries.

Important Menu Terms

Vegan
A dish prepared without animal-derived ingredients. Indian Accent provides a dedicated vegan menu.

Chef's Special
A dish or menu specially recommended by the chef and representing the restaurant's culinary style.

Gluten-Free
Food prepared without gluten-containing ingredients. Indian Accent provides a dedicated gluten-free menu.

Bestseller
A popular dish that is frequently selected by customers.

Combo
A combination of multiple food or beverage items offered together.
