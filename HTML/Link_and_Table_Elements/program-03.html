Practical Class Preparation

Students should follow these steps before attending a practical class:

  1. Read the topic or practical task provided by the faculty.
  2. Review the concepts related to the practical session.
  3. Bring the required notebook, lab record, and other academic materials.
  4. Reach the laboratory before the practical class begins.
  5. Follow the instructions given by the faculty member.
  6. Complete the assigned practical work and save the required files.
  7. Submit the practical record or assignment as instructed.

Important Academic Terms

Lecture
A teaching session where a faculty member explains theoretical concepts and course material to students.
Lab
A practical session where students apply theoretical concepts through programming, experiments, or hands-on activities.
Tutorial
An academic session focused on solving problems, discussing concepts, and clearing students' doubts.
Internal Assessment
An evaluation conducted during the semester through tests, assignments, presentations, attendance, projects, or other academic activities.
Practical
A hands-on academic activity in which students apply their knowledge through experiments, programming tasks, or other practical exercises.
