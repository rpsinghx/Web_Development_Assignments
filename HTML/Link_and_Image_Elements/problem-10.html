About Institution

GLA University is a private university in Uttar Pradesh offering undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programs in several areas of education. The university provides students with opportunities to develop academic knowledge, practical skills and professional capabilities.

The university provides an environment where students can participate in academic activities, technical projects, sports, cultural events and student clubs. Its programs are designed to connect classroom learning with practical applications.

Students are encouraged to develop innovation, communication, teamwork and problem-solving skills throughout their academic journey.

Principal / Director Message

The university leadership encourages students to focus on both academic excellence and personal development. Students are motivated to become responsible professionals who can contribute positively to society.

"Education becomes meaningful when knowledge is combined with curiosity, discipline and practical experience."

The university encourages every student to explore new ideas and participate actively in learning beyond the classroom.

Academic Programs

Bachelor of Technology

GLA University offers various B.Tech programs in engineering and technology. Students can study areas such as Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Information Technology and other engineering disciplines.

The B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning program focuses on programming, mathematics, machine learning, artificial intelligence and data-driven technologies.

For example, students may study concepts such as H2O in scientific applications and mathematical expressions such as x2 during their academic journey.

Campus Facilities

The university campus provides facilities designed to support academic learning and student development. Students have access to classrooms, laboratories, library resources and other facilities required for their studies.

The library provides students with access to books, study materials and digital resources. Laboratories provide opportunities for students to gain hands-on practical experience.

Practical learning is an important part of university education.

Student Life

Student life at the university extends beyond classrooms. Students can participate in sports, cultural activities, technical events, competitions and different student clubs.

These activities help students develop leadership, teamwork, communication and organizational skills. Participation in extracurricular activities can also provide students with opportunities to meet people with similar interests.

University life is not only about completing a degree; it is also about developing experiences and discovering new interests.
