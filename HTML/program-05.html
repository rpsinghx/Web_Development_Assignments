Annual College Fest 2026

Fest Introduction

Annual College Fest 2026 is a three-day college festival organized at IIT Delhi. Students can participate in different competitions and events during the fest.

Rules

  • Participants must follow the instructions of the coordinator.
  • Participants must report to the venue on time.
  • Participants must maintain discipline during the fest.
  • The decision of the judges will be final.
  • Participants must follow the rules of their respective competition.

Definitions

Open Event
An event open to eligible participants.
Team Event
An event in which participants compete as a team.
Individual Event
An event in which one participant competes individually.
Registration
The process of entering a participant into an event.
Coordinator
A person responsible for managing an event.
Final Round
The last round of a competition.
