McDonald's

About Us

McDonald's is one of the world's most recognized fast-food restaurant brands. It is known for serving burgers, fries, beverages, desserts and other popular meals.

McDonald's focuses on providing convenient meals in a friendly environment. Its menu includes a variety of choices for breakfast, lunch and snacks.

Signature Items

Big Mac

The Big Mac is one of McDonald's most famous burgers, made with layers of beef, fresh vegetables, cheese and its signature sauce.

French Fries

McDonald's French Fries are a popular side item enjoyed with burgers and other meals.

Coca-Cola

A chilled Coca-Cola is a popular beverage choice to accompany a McDonald's meal.

Special Offer

Enjoy a great-value meal combination! Customers can choose a burger, fries and a refreshing drink as part of selected meal options.

Offers and menu items may vary by restaurant and location.

Opening Information

McDonald's restaurant hours can vary depending on the location. Many restaurants operate for extended hours, while some locations may provide 24-hour service.
