Harvard University

Admission Overview

Harvard University is a prestigious private university located in Cambridge, Massachusetts, USA. Founded in 1636, it is one of the oldest institutions of higher education in the United States.

This admission handbook provides a simplified overview of the undergraduate admission process, eligibility requirements, required documents, important terms, and an illustrative fee structure. Application Deadline: 1 January 2027

The Admissions Office encourages students to explore their academic interests, prepare their application carefully, and submit all required materials before the deadline.


Eligibilty

Students applying for undergraduate admission should meet the university's admission requirements.

  • Applicants must have completed or be in the process of completing secondary/high school education.
  • Applicants must submit accurate academic records.
  • Applicants should demonstrate strong academic preparation.
  • Applicants may need to provide standardized testing information according to the admission policy applicable to their application cycle.
  • International students must provide the documents required for international admission.
  • All application information must be truthful and complete.

Application Process

Applicants should follow these steps in sequence:

  1. Explore the undergraduate programs and admission requirements.
  2. Create an account on the appropriate application platform.
  3. Complete the personal and academic information sections.
  4. Select the desired program or academic area.
  5. Prepare and submit the required essays and application materials.
  6. Upload or arrange for the required academic documents.
  7. Submit the application before the applicable deadline.
  8. Pay the application fee or request a fee waiver if eligible.
  9. Monitor the application portal for updates or additional requirements.
  10. Wait for the admission decision and follow the instructions provided if admitted.

Required Documents

Applicants may need to provide:

  • High school academic transcripts
  • Completed application form
  • Personal essays
  • Teacher recommendations
  • Counselor/school report
  • Valid identification or passport information for international applicants
  • Standardized test information, where applicable
  • Financial aid documents, if applying for financial assistance
  • English-language proficiency information, where required
  • Additional documents requested by the university

Important Terms

    Eligibilty
    The conditions and qualifications that an applicant must satisfy to be considered for admission.

    Merit List
    A list or ranking of applicants based on academic performance or other admission criteria. The exact use of merit lists varies between universities.

    Counselling
    Guidance provided to students during the admission process to help them understand programs, requirements, financial aid, and enrollment procedures.

    Scholarship
    Financial assistance awarded to eligible students to help support their educational expenses.

    Registration
    The process of providing the required information and formally enrolling or applying through the university's designated system.
