Welcome to the Festival

Experience delicious food, traditional recipes and cultural performances from different regions of India. The Grand Food & Culture Festival 2026 brings together food lovers, families and artists to celebrate the rich food and cultural heritage of our country. Visitors can enjoy tasty dishes, live cooking shows, traditional performances, exciting competitions and music throughout the three-day festival.
